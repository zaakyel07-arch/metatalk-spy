How to Play Meta Talk Spy

1. Pass the Phone

The Table Moderator enters 4–6 player names and starts the round. Pass the phone around so each player privately views their role.

2. Civilians vs. The Spy

Civilians all see the exact same Category and Secret Word (e.g., Places: Airport Terminal).

1 Spy sees ONLY the Category—they do not know the Secret Word!

3. Ask Clever Questions (5 Mins)

Players take turns asking each other one question about the secret word (e.g., "Would you visit this place early in the morning?").

• Civilians: Prove you know the word without making it too obvious for the Spy.

• The Spy: Listen carefully, blend in, and deduce the secret word!

4. Vote & Final Guess

When the 5-minute timer ends (or earlier if ready), everyone counts 3-2-1 and points to who they think the Spy is. If the Spy is caught, they get one final chance to guess the Secret Word to steal the win!
